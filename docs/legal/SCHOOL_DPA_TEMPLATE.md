# School data processing agreement — drafting schedule

Complete and review with the school and Zimbabwe advisers before execution. Roles follow actual purposes, not a contract label alone.

1. **Parties and roles:** Identify the school controller and ChemWiz processor for school-directed learning records, plus any separate controller activities such as the operator’s invoicing/security.
2. **Processing details:** Specify educational purposes, documented instructions, duration, people affected, data categories, permitted reports and optional AI/voice arrangements. Personal data rights remain with the affected individuals.
3. **Authority and notices:** Allocate notice delivery, parent/legal guardian authority and written consent verification, age assurance, learner explanations, adulthood review and withdrawal. School payment/teacher approval is not itself guardian authority.
4. **Access/security:** Define platform support access, school administrator/teacher/class roles, member confirmation, confidentiality, authentication, access revocation, log controls, patching, backup/recovery and export safeguards. Describe tested measures.
5. **Providers/transfers:** Maintain the actual provider list, purposes, countries, access, retention and agreements; define subprocessor approval/change notices and any required transfer assessments/authorisations. Do not equate an API storage flag with provider-wide zero retention.
6. **Rights and cooperation:** Identify contacts, verification procedures, request routing, correction/deletion/objection handling, lawful exceptions, school cooperation and response tracking.
7. **Incidents:** Define immediate internal escalation and cooperation that allow applicable regulator/individual notifications. Include contact chains, evidence preservation and a breach register.
8. **Human review:** No solely automated school decisions affecting a child’s rights; define authorised human review and challenge procedures.
9. **Retention/exit:** Agree export scope/format, requester authority, school access end, production deletion/return, external evidence, backup expiry, legal holds and confirmation. No automatic cross-school transfer of learner records.
10. **Assurance:** Agree proportionate audit evidence, risk/DPIA reviews, staff training and regulatory cooperation. Record the signed schedule version with the school subscription.

Attach the current processing inventory, approved security schedule, subprocessor/transfer register, retention schedule and incident contacts. Counsel must settle final contractual wording and responsibilities against the actual deployment.
